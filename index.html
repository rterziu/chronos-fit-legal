Point of sale

Demo available · not yet certified

Point-of-sale software designed for fiscalization in Kosovo, with the accounting, declarations and payroll in the same program.

  • A sale screen built for speed: search, tap, pay
  • Designed to keep selling offline and report coupons once the connection is back
  • Sales and purchase books, declarations and payroll, ready to check and enter in EDI
  • Invoices to businesses, stock and fixed assets
  • For Windows, in Albanian, English, Serbian and Turkish. Android and iOS are coming soon
