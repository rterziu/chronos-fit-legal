Not yet certified. Chronos POS has not yet been certified by the Tax Administration of Kosovo, so it must not be used to issue fiscal coupons for real sales. The demo issues none: nothing reaches ATK, and every coupon it prints is marked as having no fiscal value.

What it does

From the first sale to the month’s declarations.

At the counter

A sale in a few taps

Search by name or barcode, tap the article, choose how it was paid. Best sellers are one tap away, and on Windows the till can be worked from the keyboard.

Discounts and split payments

A discount on one article or on the whole basket, and one sale paid by cash, card and voucher together, with the change worked out.

Quantities as you measure them

Kilograms and grams, litres and millilitres, hours and minutes, each in the article’s own unit.

The fiscal coupon

Laid out after ATK’s samples

Signed, with its QR code and its numbers. It prints on 58 and 80 mm rolls, always in Albanian, and a second print is marked as a copy.

Returns and cancellations

Nothing is edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected by a new coupon that refers to the first, and coupons from the paper block can be entered afterwards.

Designed to sell without internet

Coupons are still issued and marked offline, then sent by themselves and in order once the connection is back. The law allows 48 hours, and the program warns as they pass.

The books

Books and declarations

Sales and purchase books, and forms TV, QS, QL, WM, CM, WR and CD laid out like ATK’s own, with a reminder before each deadline. You check them and file them in EDI.

Purchases by what they are

Investments, expenses and goods, line by line. Goods enter the stock, investments the asset register, and expenses the right box of form CD.

Payroll

Employees, the month’s pay and payslips in Excel, feeding WM and CM. Type the net pay if that is what you know, and the gross is worked out.

Invoices to businesses

Invoices with credit and debit notes, on A4 or on the 80 mm roll, with or without a VAT number, and a register of customers.

Stock and fixed assets

Costs, receipts and counts, the stock list in Excel, and an asset register with depreciation for the books and for tax.

The VAT rate

The law’s list of what carries 8% wherever a rate is chosen, and a suggestion when a name such as an electricity bill looks like it. It never changes a rate by itself.

Control and safety

Who can do what

Owner, accountant and cashier, each with their own PIN and permissions. A till left alone signs out by itself, and every action is logged.

Internal controls

Eleven rules raise alarms on cancellations, discounts, stock and gaps in sales, with a monthly report.

Your data stays with you

Records are encrypted on your own computer, and no server of ours holds them. A backup is made each day and on closing, to the folder you choose, sealed with your password.

The program

What you will see.

Screens from the Windows program, in its demo mode. The business, names and figures are practice data.

How it is used

From installing it to closing the month.

Install it

Download the installer and run it. Windows may warn you, because it is not signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Set it up once

The owner answers once: language, business, taxes, the till and their own PIN. It starts in Demo, where nothing reaches ATK.

Add your articles

Name, price with VAT, VAT rate, unit and category from ATK’s tables. The program points out where the law gives 8%.

Sell

Sign in with your PIN, tap the articles, press Pay and issue the coupon. In a certified till it goes to ATK first and is printed after.

Close the day

The day’s report shows the sales by payment and by VAT rate. Close the shift and see that every coupon has been reported.

Close the month

The Accounting place walks you through six steps: coupons sent, purchases entered, generate, fix and approve, save for EDI, mark as filed.

The licence

One licence for each till.

Licences will be issued once Chronos POS is certified. This is how they will work.

Try it first

Demo and Test run for 30 days on a computer with no licence. Production needs one from the first coupon.

How a till gets one

The owner sends the till’s code and the business’s NUI, both under Settings, Setup, Licence, and pastes back the code they receive. No server of ours is involved.

For how long

A month, six months or a year. A renewal carries on from the old end, so no days are gained or lost.

A new computer

It counts as a new till and gets a new code. Your records come back with your backup.

When it ends

The owner hears 30 days before and everyone 7 days before. Everything works for 15 more days, or 7 on a licence of a month or two. Then new sales, invoices and records stop.

What never stops

Sending coupons in the queue, returns, cancellations and credit notes, the history and reprints, reports, books and declarations, exports and backups. Nothing is deleted.

What it needs

A Windows computer and, for the coupon, a printer.

64-bit Windows

Tried on Windows 11. Android is to follow and is not available yet.

Internet

To report coupons to ATK. It keeps selling without it, within the 48 hours the law allows.

A printer

A thermal printer for 58 or 80 mm rolls with a Windows driver. Invoices also print on A4, and coupons can be saved as PDF.

Not for every business

Fuel stations and taxis need certified hardware, which it does not support.

Questions

What people ask first.

Is Chronos POS certified?

Not yet. It has to pass the application to the Tax Administration of Kosovo first, with testing against its service. Until then it must not be used for real sales.

What can I do with the demo?

Try everything with practice data for 30 days: sales, coupons, books and payroll. Nothing reaches ATK and no coupon has fiscal value.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer, encrypted. Backups go to a folder you choose, sealed with your own password. We hold none of it.

Does it file my declarations?

No. It prepares them, you check them, and you file them in EDI with your own account. The only thing the program sends to ATK is the fiscal coupon.

What happens if my computer breaks?

Install the program on the new one and restore your backup there. The new computer counts as a new till, so it needs a new licence code.

What happens if I do not renew?

After the days of grace new sales stop, but your records stay yours: you can still read them, report on them, export them and back them up.

Try it on your own computer.

The demo needs no licence and nothing you enter leaves your computer.
