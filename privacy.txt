========================================================================

WHAT THIS APP STORES

Your email address, the answers you gave during setup, the workouts you
log, your body measurements, your daily check-ins, the food and water
you log, the supplements you tick off, and any feedback you send. That
is all of it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no third-party
analytics. The app does not read your location, your contacts, your
photos, or data from Apple Health. Nothing about you is sold or shared
with anyone.

WHERE IT IS STORED

On your phone, and on servers operated by Supabase inside the European
Union. Each row is locked to your account by database-level security, so
no other user can read your data even if they tried.

HEALTH INFORMATION

Body weight, measurements, soreness and pain scores are sensitive. They
are collected only because the training features need them, and only for
as long as you keep your account.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS

You can export everything to a file, or delete your account and all of
it, from the Profile screen. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

QUESTIONS

Use the feedback option in Profile and the message reaches the developer
directly.

========================================================================

TERMS OF USE

THIS IS NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

Chronos Fit is a training and nutrition tool. It does not diagnose,
treat, or give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor
or a physiotherapist. The pain features adjust your training and tell
you to seek a professional; they never tell you what is wrong.

TRAINING CARRIES RISK

You train at your own risk. Speak to a qualified professional before
starting, especially if you have an injury, pain, or a health condition.
Stop if something hurts.

YOUR ACCOUNT

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens
under your account.

EARLY SOFTWARE

This app is in active development. Features will change, and while your
logged workouts are saved both on your phone and on the server, no
software is immune to loss.

NO WARRANTY

The app is provided as it is, without guarantees that it will be
uninterrupted, error-free, or that its recommendations will suit you.
