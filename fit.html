What it does

Everything a session needs. Nothing it does not.

Programs that progress

Every set you log moves the next target. Weight, reps and rest are worked out for you, and all of it is yours to change.

More than the gym

Calisthenics progressions, running plans from Couch to 5K to a half marathon, cycling and mobility, side by side in one week.

Nutrition without the fuss

Calories, protein and water against a target that follows your goal, with the meals you eat most saved for one tap.

Works without signal

Everything is saved on your phone first and synced when you are back online.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Export or delete your data whenever you like.

Ten languages

English, Albanian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Dutch and Arabic.

How it works

Three steps, then it is habit.

Tell it your goal

Your goal, your days and your equipment build the plan.

Train and log in one tap

Targets are filled in already. Tap when the set is done.

Come back to a better plan

The next session is built from what you actually did.

Your training data is yours.

No ads and no trackers. Export everything or delete your account from inside the app.
