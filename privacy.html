Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026Shqip

What this app stores

Your email address, the answers you gave during setup, the workouts you log, your body measurements, your daily check-ins, the food and water you log, the supplements you tick off, any progress photos you add, and any feedback you send. That is all of it.

What it does not do

There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics. The app does not read your location, your contacts, your photos, or data from Apple Health. Nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and on servers operated by Supabase inside the European Union. Each row is locked to your account by database-level security, so no other user can read your data even if they tried.

Health information

Body weight, measurements, soreness and pain scores are sensitive, and progress photos more so. Photos are kept in a private area of the server that is never publicly readable, opened only through a link that expires after a minute, and reachable only by the account that uploaded them. All of it is collected because the training features need it, and only for as long as you keep your account.

Your data is yours

You can export everything to a file, or delete your account and all of it, from the Profile screen. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Questions

Use the feedback option in Profile and the message reaches the developer directly.

Terms of use

This is not medical advice

Chronos Fit is a training and nutrition tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or a physiotherapist. The pain features adjust your training and tell you to seek a professional; they never tell you what is wrong.

Training carries risk

You train at your own risk. Speak to a qualified professional before starting, especially if you have an injury, pain, or a health condition. Stop if something hurts.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

Early software

This app is in active development. Features will change, and while your logged workouts are saved both on your phone and on the server, no software is immune to loss.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is, without guarantees that it will be uninterrupted, error-free, or that its recommendations will suit you.
